Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For senior sessions, we recommend booking 4–8 weeks in advance, especially for spring and fall. Product photography can often be scheduled with shorter notice.

How long will my session take?

Sessions typically run 1–2 hours depending on your package, locations selected, and the number of outfit changes. We'll discuss the details when you book.

When will I receive my photos?

You'll receive your fully edited gallery within 2–3 weeks of your session. We prioritize fast turnarounds and will let you know your estimated delivery date up front.

Do you offer digital files and prints?

Yes! All sessions include a high-resolution digital gallery. We also offer premium print packages, canvases, and albums — ask us about options when you book.
